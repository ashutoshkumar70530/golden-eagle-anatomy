## Item 1 — Recognize the bill
**Objective:** Identify external structures  
**Mission:** The Hunter’s Head  
**Type:** Image-based multiple choice  
**Prompt:** “Select the Golden Eagle’s strongly hooked bill.”  
**Options:** A. Bill; B. Nape; C. Tail; D. Primary feathers  
**Correct:** A  
**Feedback:** “Correct. The bill is the hooked structure at the front of the head.”  
**Source:** Cornell Lab of Ornithology, Golden Eagle identification — https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Golden_Eagle/id

## Item 3 — Separate structure from location

**Correct:** A  
**Feedback:** “The bill is at the front of the head. The nape is at the back of the neck; rectrices are tail feathers; the hallux is the rear digit.”
