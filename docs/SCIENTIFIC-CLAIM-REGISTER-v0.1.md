# Golden Eagle Anatomy — Scientific Claim Register v0.1

**Purpose:** Prevent plausible-sounding but unsupported anatomy statements from entering the course.  
**Status:** Draft claims for verification; this is not a completed expert review.  
**Species:** Golden Eagle (*Aquila chrysaetos*)

## Release rule

A claim can be marked **approved** only when:
- the source directly supports the wording;
- any qualifier is preserved in narration and on-screen text;
- the selected image does not contradict or obscure the claim;
- the assessment item tests the claim at the intended beginner level;
- a qualified reviewer has checked the anatomy-specific labels.

## Known science risks to prevent

- Calling the nape the top of the head rather than the back of the neck.
- Treating juvenile plumage marks as universal or as an exact age indicator.
- Mixing up primary feathers, secondary feathers, and tail feathers.
- Confusing the feathered tarsus/leg, digits, and talons.
- Labeling a hallux without showing which way the foot is oriented.
- Presenting generated images as anatomical evidence.
- Presenting simplified skeletal art as a complete or species-verified Golden Eagle skeleton.
