# Golden Eagle Anatomy — Science Source Register

**Purpose:** Track sources used to verify species facts, anatomical terminology, function explanations, and visual references.  
**Status:** Initial research register; each learner-facing claim must be linked to a specific source and reviewed.

| Source | Best use | Limitations / checks |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell Lab of Ornithology — Golden Eagle overview: https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Golden_Eagle | General species overview, visible characteristics, behavior, identification context | Not a detailed anatomical atlas; do not infer internal anatomy from overview text |
| Cornell Lab of Ornithology — Golden Eagle identification: https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Golden_Eagle/id | Plumage, silhouette, wing/tail appearance, age-related visual differences | Age and plumage variation must be represented accurately; avoid universal claims from one photo |
| U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service — Golden Eagle: https://www.fws.gov/species/golden-eagle-aquila-chrysaetos | Species characteristics and general physical description | General profile, not a detailed functional anatomy source |
| U.S. National Park Service — Golden Eagle: https://www.nps.gov/brca/learn/nature/golden-eagle.htm | General identification and natural-history context | Check individual statements against more specialized sources where necessary |
| American Bird Conservancy — Golden Eagle: https://abcbirds.org/birds/golden-eagle/ | General identification and physical characteristics | Supplement for specialist anatomical terminology and skeletal detail |

## Verification protocol

For each proposed learner-facing statement, record:
- Exact claim / label
- Source title and direct URL
- Relevant section, page, figure, or passage
- Whether the source supports the **name**, **function**, **species-specific appearance**, or **illustration**
- Confidence / unresolved ambiguity
- Reviewer and review date
- Final wording used in the course

A source confirming that a structure exists does not automatically validate a diagram's proportions, location, internal relationship, or function. For skeletal cutaways and detailed foot anatomy, use a reputable ornithology/anatomy reference or qualified reviewer.

## Asset rights protocol

For every image or illustration, separately record:
- Asset filename and intended screen
- Creator / photographer
- Original source URL
- License and evidence of license
- Required attribution
- Modifications made
- Permission for redistribution in a public web demo and in a SCORM package

Do not place assets in the public repository until redistribution rights are confirmed. Keep private working files and unlicensed reference images out of the repository.
