Your expedition

Three missions. One remarkable bird.

Explore each region, inspect the visible structures, then prove what you’ve learned in a short challenge.

⌁
MISSION 01

The Hunter’s Head

Explore the bill, eye, crown, and nape. Learn what can—and cannot—be identified from a close-up.

⟋
MISSION 02

The Flight System

Inspect the wing, flight-feather groups, and tail. Connect visible structure with flight, carefully.

⌘
MISSION 03

The Capture System

Distinguish the feathered leg, digits, and talons, with a small amount of supporting skeletal context.

How the lab works

  • Observe a verified image or reviewed diagram.
  • Reveal focused labels and magnified details.
  • Answer a short challenge with explanatory feedback.
  • Review and retry within the course rules.

Expedition completion

Complete all three mission challenges and pass the final 10-question assessment with at least 70% to complete the learning journey.
